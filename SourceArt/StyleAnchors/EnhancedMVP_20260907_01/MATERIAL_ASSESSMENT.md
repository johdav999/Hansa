# Material assessment

The Bakery is the material-family anchor: weathered oak, lime plaster/mortar, brick and clay, limestone, forged iron, lead roof, glass, flour linen, and functionally localized soot. Its generated base colors are useful only with their real normal/roughness sets and must not be used to fake lighting.

The Mill establishes a restrained related set: fieldstone, masonry/brick, weathered and recessed timber, muted paint, forged iron, and glass. It needs texel-density and mip/gameplay-camera validation before approval.

The road establishes a low-saturation earth surface with crown and ruts. Later pieces must share one material response across straight, corner, T, cross, and end states and must avoid obvious repeated unique marks.

The merchant-house proxy contributes contrast and readable material separation only. The current harbor contributes no approved material language.
