# TR-07 — Presence contributions and Merchant Office

Status date: 2026-09-20.

## Implemented contract

Foreign-presence progression is authoritative state keyed by house and city. Accepted spot trades, settled public-market route actions, and executed station orders contribute once through their immutable domain-event sequence. Positive settled physical commerce adds lawful volume and absolute transaction value; sales to the city add one delivery and only the portion that filled the city's pre-delivery reserve shortfall. Free station/home transfers, rejected or zero-settlement actions, and replayed event sequences contribute nothing. Returned goods can contribute again only after a later lawful market sale with actual value. Counters are lifetime cumulative for this stage family, have no decay, saturate at signed 64-bit maximum, and keep a 64-entry causal history. Active stations add one reliable tick per simulation tick and one solvent tick when the house can cover that station's authored upkeep.

The two-step upgrade uses `RequestPresenceUpgrade` and `FundPresenceUpgrade`. Request revalidates the authored contribution requirements. Funding revalidates the transition, progress, money, station-owned material inventory, and costs against a candidate ledger before committing any mutation. Completion occurs after the authored construction duration and grants only the target stage's authored capabilities after city-policy denials.

Rostock's Merchant Office costs 150,000 pfennig, 12,000 milli-units of planks, and 2,000 milli-units of tools. Its concrete runtime effects are the authored 50,000 milli-unit station-capacity increase and four additional standing-order slots. Station, factor, inventory, lease, route and order identities are preserved.

## UI and authoring

The native Trade Map inspector exposes exact contribution totals, next-stage requirement ratios, unmet labels, capability differences, request/fund/construction state, and recent history. `TradeMap.Presence.Progress` and `TradeMap.Presence.Upgrade` provide semantic automation and ordinary focusable controls. Eligible, blocked, unaffordable, suspended and construction states derive from the authoritative projection, so they do not create alert spam.

The existing generic Authoring Studio reflection, validation, hashing and impact-analysis path now covers every new stage requirement, construction duration, and city-policy capacity/order bonus through complete `UPROPERTY` metadata. Reversed discovery remains deterministic because the compiled registry hashes these fields.
