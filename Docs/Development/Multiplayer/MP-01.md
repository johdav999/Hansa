## Audit summary

- `EHansaGameplayCommandType` has three test-only and 15 production commands; `EHansaClientIntentType` has only three.
- `AHansaGameMode` selects the primary or one rival house; a third through eighth distinct house cannot be assigned.
- Runtime validation expects one player plus an optional rival, and AI control is coupled to the rival house.
- HUD, build, trade, research, inspector, ship, time, and save flows still access the local `UHansaRuntimeSimulationHost` in multiple paths.
- Save discovery is one local `manual.hansa` plus one local `autosave.hansa`.
- Remote projections lack production, population, market-history, logistics, navigation, permission, and session data required by normal screens.
- No online-service SDK/configuration was detected.

## Provider decision

EOS is selected behind Hansa-owned adapters, using Online Subsystem EOS for UE 5.8. Account Portal with Epic Account Services/Connect is the default identity path; EOS Sessions/Lobbies and P2P relay cover internet discovery/invites/connectivity. LAN/direct retains a distinct offline trust model. Official links and rationale are in [ReleaseContract.md](ReleaseContract.md).

External prerequisites are an Epic Developer Portal organization/product, deployments/client policy, test accounts, and credentials. They block MP-10 live acceptance only. No secret or provider configuration was added.
