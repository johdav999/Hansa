# TR-02 — Trade-presence definitions and authoritative state foundation

Status: **Verified** on 2026-09-20 for the TR-02 scope. This prompt adds authorable definitions, immutable compiled data, authoritative house/city state, read projections, deterministic persistence, migration, and catalog evidence. It intentionally adds no establishment, upgrade, construction, station-order, plot-use, privilege, or governance command and therefore changes no trade or market behavior.

## Seeded ladder and policies

The six authored stages are `VisitingContact`, `TradeStation`, `MerchantOffice`, `MerchantQuarter`, `PrivilegedPresence`, and scenario-gated `ExceptionalGovernance`. Costs, contribution thresholds, predecessor stages, granted capabilities, plot caps, building caps, and station requirements are data rather than command logic.

Four current-city policies exist for Lübeck, Hamburg, Lüneburg, and Rostock. Rostock alone supplies the historical opening stage, `PresenceStage.VisitingContact`. Ordinary policies disallow exceptional governance; no amount of contribution volume can bypass that policy.

The catalog contains 14 typed capabilities. They are descriptive grants in TR-02: no pipeline system consumes them to create cargo, money, buildings, market stock, routes, station orders, plots, privileges, or governance.

## Production assets

Twenty-four production `UPrimaryDataAsset` records are stored below `Content/Hansa/Core/TradePresence/`:

- `Capabilities/`: 14 capability definitions.
- `Stages/`: 6 stage definitions.
- `Policies/`: 4 city-policy definitions.

The asset manager scans all three primary asset types. The commandlet seed is deterministic and authors missing records without replacing reviewed assets unless replacement is explicitly requested.
