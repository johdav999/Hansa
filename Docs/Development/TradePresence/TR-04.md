## Implementation decisions

- A station is keyed by stable station, inventory, factor, and leased-plot IDs. Gameplay identity never depends on a provider task, filename, actor, or asset path.
- A proposal reserves one authored commercial site. Funding consumes the authored stage money and goods atomically through the inventory ledger. Completion occurs during the deterministic construction phase at the authored tick.
- Station storage is a distinct house station inventory and begins empty. Cargo and reservations block voluntary closure.
- Construction cancellation refunds the authored basis-point fraction to the original funding inventory and house. Completed closure has no refund and releases the lease.
- Closing restores the authored city opening stage while retaining the closed station record as audit/history state.
- Rostock continues to use its ordinary autonomous market and remote-industry simulation outside the leased station site.
- World presentation reuses the user-approved `/Game/Mesh/hansa-harbor/BP_Harbor_Review` asset. No Engine primitive or unapproved generated mesh is used.
