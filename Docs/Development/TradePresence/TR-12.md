# TR-12 — Multi-city rollout and regional-economy integration

Status: implemented 2026-09-21. Deterministic catalog, scale-profile, responsive semantic UI, and Lübeck-exclusion evidence pass. A single assembled real-viewport journey that combines a newly authored abstract-city presence with station orders and a player route remains a later UAT gate; abstract cities intentionally have no station site or visit/build action.

## Delivered contract

The regional authoring draft now has a reviewed trade-presence subset on top of the existing thirty-city, five-region economy. Lübeck is explicitly rendered/buildable, Rostock rendered/visitable, and Hamburg, Lüneburg, Wismar, and Stralsund explicitly market-only with reviewed map coordinates. Wismar, Stralsund, Danzig, and Bergen receive public-market, report, and route-access visiting-contact policies. They receive no station site, leased plot, specialization, privilege, city project, visit action, or construction right.

`EHansaCityPresentationClass` is compiled into the immutable registry with authored map coordinates. The compiler rejects a station site unless its city has a non-empty market, public access, route connectivity, an allowed stage that grants `TradeStation`, and a non-market-only presentation. The validation searches the complete sorted definition set, so its outcome is independent of discovery order.

The existing Baltic route definition reaches Wismar, Stralsund, Danzig, and Bergen. These routes, foreign-presence commands, station orders, and remote production all continue to use the same city-market inventories and reports; no parallel stock or fallback supply was added. `FHansaRegionalEconomyExecutor` remains the sole automatic regional producer/exchange path and excludes `City.Lubeck` as both source and destination.

## Trade-map scale work

The trade-map projection now enumerates the registry rather than a four-city constant. It exposes:

- search over stable ID and display label;
- All, Presence, and Routes city modes;
- selected-good cycling with per-city report age and stale/unknown state;
- explicit rendered, visitable, buildable, market-only, presence, and route capability fields;
- at most 48 materialized map markers;
- a 20-row route window with ordinary Previous/Next controls;
- stable semantic IDs and keyboard/controller focus for filters, search, goods, and paging.

Existing approved Trade Presence map art is reused. The new filters, search field, and paging affordances are native Slate controls, so no unreviewed bitmap was introduced.

## Performance and determinism evidence

`Hansa.Integration.Authoring.RegionalProduction.ScaleProfile` runs a real thirty-city scenario for 240 deterministic ticks, inspects every in-flight regional shipment for the two-sided Lübeck exclusion, builds the trade-map projection, performs 200 search/filter updates, and encodes a save. The passing run recorded:

- 240 ticks including a projection inspection after every tick: 6274.01 ms;
- initial trade-map projection: 0.42 ms;
- 200 search/filter updates: 4.18 ms;
- save encode: 68.40 ms and 237,423 bytes;
- 30 initial markers and 9 initial route rows, below the 48/20 materialization bounds.

These are development automation measurements, not Shipping percentile claims. The test asserts deterministic success and structural/size bounds; it records timings for regression comparison without using machine-sensitive timing thresholds.

`Hansa.Integration.Authoring.RegionalProduction.Catalog` proves thirty markets, five six-city regions, ten production chains, eight city policies, truthful reviewed classifications, route reachability, abstract-policy restrictions, compiler validity, and identical registry hashes after reversed discovery.
