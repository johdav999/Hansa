# TR-09 — Leased plots and bounded foreign construction

Status: authoritative gameplay and projection layer implemented; focused automation verified 2026-09-21. Real viewport binding/capture remains a presentation follow-up and is not claimed here.

## Delivered contract

Merchant Quarter presence can construct only approved building categories wholly inside an active, authored lease. The ordinary `PlaceBuilding` command, placement validator, construction cost executor, inventory ledger, event publication, rollback, save codec, and determinism hash remain the single authoritative path. Foreign construction spends the player's money and the station inventory; it does not appropriate city inventory, roads, buildings, or land outside the lease.

Authored station sites now compile stable bounds and permitted categories. Runtime leases preserve city, house, station, site, bounds, categories, occupancy IDs, and active state. Placement emits distinct failures for missing, suspended, out-of-bounds, wrong-category, and insufficient-stage rights. Cancellation/removal clears lease occupancy, while station closure rejects until leased buildings are removed.

Save format 19 and determinism fingerprint 31 include the bounded lease contract. Format 18 migrates through `Hansa.Save.18To19.AddBoundedForeignConstructionRights`, hydrating rights from the authored station site. Gameplay command schema remains 13 and multiplayer client-intent schema remains 7.
