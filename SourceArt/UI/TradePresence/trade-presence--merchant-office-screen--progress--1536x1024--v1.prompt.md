## Final prompt

Create a polished game UI concept image for Hansa, a historically grounded Hanseatic city-building and trade strategy game. Show the Baltic Trade Map screen at 1536x1024 in a 3:2 landscape frame. Preserve a restrained late-medieval material language: dark navy chart field, warm parchment information surfaces, oxidized brass frames, muted teal sea, terracotta warning accents, ivory text, subtle worn paper and metal texture, crisp modern information hierarchy. Unified compact top status bar. Main left area is a generalized Baltic chart with Lübeck and Rostock, a route line and a small cog marker. Right side is a tall Foreign Presence inspector for Rostock at the Trade Station stage advancing to Merchant Office. Clearly separate: current stage and status; authoritative contribution progress rows for lawful delivered volume, transaction value, city shortage relief, completed deliveries, reliable operation, solvency and investment; exact current/required values; requirement states with icon plus label, not color alone; a capability-difference section showing larger station storage and four additional standing-order slots; an upgrade review showing 150,000 pfennig, 12 units of planks, 2 units of tools, and 3 construction ticks; a prominent Request review or Fund office action; a compact construction progress state and a chronological contribution/upgrade history. Dynamic text remains conceptual UI reference, not production raster content. Include disabled, warning and eligible states through native-looking controls. Avoid fantasy ornament, gold excess, modern glass UI, illegible microtext, photorealistic people, copyrighted game layouts, and decorative clutter. Make the right inspector feel practical, auditable, and readable at game scale.
