# Audit iterations

1. Initial batch audit opened each `.blend` headlessly and exposed source unit, bounds, mesh, UV, material, packed-image, and transform data.
2. Review-ground helper geometry was excluded from render-asset bounds so Bakery measurements represent the asset rather than the review scene.
3. Road QA-floor geometry was excluded and transform reporting was reduced to actionable samples. Final JSON evidence was regenerated with Blender 3.5.1.
4. Unreal assets were measured directly and reviewed in the Lübeck MVP level and PIE lighting. Contract dispositions were then set without modifying or promoting candidate assets.
