## Deterministic phase trace

The pipeline order is:

1. `ApplyCommands`
2. `CalendarAndWorldEvents`
3. `VehicleMovementAndTransfers`
4. `WarehousesAndStorage`
5. `ConstructionAndProduction`
6. `WorkforceAndNeeds`
7. `MarketClearing`
8. `PricesAndHistory`
9. `FinanceAndContracts`
10. `ResearchPoliticsAndVictory`
11. `PublishAndChecksum`

Route movement, upkeep, arrival, cargo transfer, and legacy foreign settlement all occur in `VehicleMovementAndTransfers`. The market observes the changed inventory later in the same tick. Publication and the determinism checksum occur last.

## Lübeck–Rostock route trace

1. The trade creator/runtime host produces a `CreateRoute` command containing a stable route ID, owned Cog, `Route.BalticSea`, ordered stops, and ordered load/unload actions.
2. The gateway validates command identity/sequence/tick/house, Cog ownership and availability, route definitions and research, the Cog cargo inventory, both city identities, cyclic reachability, goods, positive quantities, and reserve bounds against copied candidate state.
3. The accepted route enters `AtStop` with pending actions. During the same tick's vehicle phase, the Lübeck load calls `FHansaInventoryLedger::TryTransfer` from eligible city inventory to the Cog. Because Lübeck is not `bMarketOnly`, unit price and settlement remain zero. `RouteCargoTransferred` is emitted, then the Cog departs.
4. Each traveling vehicle phase charges 2 raw money units in the focused fixture, updates progress, and decrements travel ticks. Arrival changes the Cog's city, increments completed legs, schedules stop actions, and emits `RouteArrived`.
5. On the following vehicle phase, the Rostock unload again performs a physical inventory transfer. Because compiled Rostock is `bMarketOnly`, it samples the current market price, applies transaction friction, computes rounded proceeds, changes house money, stores price/settlement in `LastTransfer`, and emits `RouteTradeSettled` plus the cargo outcome event.
6. The later market phases read the resulting Rostock stock and may move the next price/report; they do not reprice the completed settlement.
7. Vehicle/route/market/inventory/house projections expose the result. The state, last transfer, movement records, event counters, price, money, and command history participate in save/hash coverage.

## Documentation disagreement resolved

Executable code and preserved-fish evidence are authoritative: current foreign `bMarketOnly` route actions **do change money**. A foreign load is a purchase and a foreign unload is a sale. A load/unload at Lübeck is an unpriced inventory transfer. `trade.md`, the older `TradeRoutes.md` route-creator note, and the failing trade-creator assertion still describe the older globally transfer-only behavior.

TR-01 does not rewrite economics to match either prose branch. `Decisions.md` records the compatibility baseline and assigns the explicit-commerce/migration decision to TR-03/TR-06. Until resolved, new work must not assume that existing routes are transfer-only or add a parallel hidden settlement path.

## Price and conservation contract observed

- Foreign settlement requires a house, matching city/good market, and positive current price.
- Base transaction friction is 500 basis points minus an applicable research reduction.
- Effective unit price uses half-away-from-zero division: `105%` for buy, `95%` for sell at baseline friction.
- Purchase affordability is bounded before transfer. Total purchase cost rounds up; total sale proceeds round toward zero.
- Capacity, available/unreserved city stock, protected quantity, minimum source reserve, carried stock, and destination free capacity bound physical quantity.
- Physical transfer completes before the signed money effect is applied. This is deterministic but not a general atomic transaction object; TP-BLOCK-002 must be resolved before direct commerce expands the failure surface.
- Partial and missed outcomes remain explicit. Local transfers retain zero unit price and zero settlement.
