# TR-11 — AI merchant parity and competition

Status: authoritative controller, normal-command parity, authored tuning, safe explanations, and focused automation implemented 2026-09-21. Full packaged UAT and the repository-wide AI suite remain separate gates.

## Delivered contract

`FHansaMerchantAIController` now considers direct public trade, route activation, station proposal and funding, station orders, presence upgrade request and funding, specialization selection, research, and owned production. Every selected action is emitted as the same closed `FHansaGameplayCommand` used by player input and is accepted or rejected by `FHansaGameplayCommandGateway`; the controller has no settlement, inventory, station, progression, privilege, or policy mutation path.

The decision boundary consumes only immutable read access, the compiled definition registry, and the owning house's lawful projections. Market choices use `CompareMarketOpportunity`, `QueryKnownMarketSupplyDemand`, and `QuerySpotTradeQuote`. Unknown or stale reports remain ineligible. Station and progression choices use owned money, vehicles, cargo, station inventory, foreign-presence projections, current capabilities, and authored city policy. Site occupancy and command ordering remain transactional gateway concerns, so an occupied site cannot be claimed twice and failed commands commit no partial goods, money, lease, order, or privilege effect.

## Determinism and risk

Options are ordered by eligibility, utility, seed/window/stable-option tie-break, and stable option ID. The existing seed contract is unchanged. New authored controls are:

- protected cash reserve;
- action cooldown;
- maximum direct-trade quantity;
- station-order target, per-update cap, and lifetime budget;
- presence-action utility.

These fields are reflected on `UHansaMerchantAITuningDefinition`, carry units/ranges, validation, migration, serialization, bulk-edit, and AI-access metadata, compile into the immutable runtime registry, participate in definition hashing, and appear through the generic Authoring Studio schema. No balance constant was added to a UI controller.

## Explanation and player visibility

`FHansaMerchantAIDecisionTrace` remains internal diagnostic controller history. `FHansaMerchantAIExplanationProjection` is the allowlisted outward projection: tick, goal, command action, public reason, accepted state, and typed gateway outcome. It contains no market values, report identities, cargo amounts, inventory IDs, station-order terms, or rival private state. `UHansaRuntimeSimulationHost::GetMerchantAIExplanationProjection` exposes that projection to diagnostics and automation.

No new player UI was added. Existing market, station-site, foreign-presence, route, and price projections already surface the effects a player may lawfully observe; the AI explanation is not inserted into ordinary player presentation.
