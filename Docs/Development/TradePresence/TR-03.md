# TR-03 — Visiting-merchant spot trade

Status: implemented and focused-verification complete on 2026-09-20.

## Delivered contract

An owned Cog physically berthed in a foreign city may submit the closed `FHansaSpotTradeCommand` for a public-market buy or sell. The authority revalidates vehicle ownership and type, location and berth state, route/travel state, navigation context, authored public-market policy, the active visiting-trade capability, good access, positive quantity, and the reviewed market update/price. The command is available through the runtime host, multiplayer client intent schema, native market inspector, semantic input, save/reload, replay, and deterministic hashing.

The UI quote is explicitly an estimate. Confirmation samples current authoritative state again and never writes a price directly. The subsequent normal market update observes the changed physical stock.

## Settlement rules

- The sampled base price is the city's current `CurrentPriceMilliMarks` for the good at command execution.
- Transaction friction is 500 basis points less applicable research reduction. Buy unit price rounds half away from zero after applying `10000 + friction`; sell unit price does the same with `10000 - friction`.
- Buy settlement rounds the total ceiling upward; sale proceeds round toward zero.
- Requested quantity is bounded deterministically. Buys are bounded by unprotected city stock, remaining Cog capacity, and available house money. Sells are bounded by carried cargo and remaining city-market capacity.
- A positive bounded amount is `Completed` when it equals the request and `Partial` otherwise. No executable quantity is `Missed`. Validation failures are typed rejections and do not mutate state.
- Inventory and money are applied to a candidate authoritative state through the inventory ledger, validated together, and committed only after all effects and the receipt succeed. Any failure discards the candidate, providing rollback without a partially committed money or cargo effect.
- The committed order is physical inventory movement, money settlement, persisted `FHansaSpotTradeRecord`, then one typed `SpotTradeCompleted`, `SpotTradePartial`, or `SpotTradeMissed` domain event. Rejected commands emit no settlement event.
- Command identities participate in the existing accepted-command ordering. A repeated identity is rejected deterministically before mutation, so replay cannot duplicate goods, money, receipt, or event.

Legacy `Load`/`Unload` route behavior is deliberately unchanged. The existing market-only-route settlement hybrid remains characterized; TR-06 owns its explicit action-kind migration or retirement.

## Native UI and semantic contract

Component inventory:

- screen shell: existing market table and selected-good detail inspector;
- navigation: existing search, filters, sortable headers, market rows, and focus order;
- panel: compact visiting-trade card shown only for an eligible foreign-city context;
- controls: buy/sell side toggle, decrement, increment, and confirm;
- status/feedback: report age, known price, available stock, requested quantity, resulting cargo/cash estimate, uncertainty text, blocker/remedy, and committed result;
- semantic actions: `Market.Detail.SpotTrade.Side`, `.Quantity.Decrease`, `.Quantity.Increase`, and `.Confirm` beneath `Market.Detail.SpotTrade`;
- decorative imagery: reference-only brass/parchment treatment; the shipping interaction is native Slate and uses no generated full-screen raster.

The card has default, hover, pressed, selected, disabled, keyboard/controller focus, warning, and error/blocker behavior through the existing native component states. Loading is represented by the containing market snapshot; no asynchronous trade transaction is introduced. Hidden cards are absent from both focus order and the semantic tree. Missing ship or access produces a disabled cause and remedy. The quote states that it is an estimate and is revalidated on confirmation.
