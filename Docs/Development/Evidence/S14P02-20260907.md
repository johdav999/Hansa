## UAT flow packets

1. Open the root HUD and inspect top navigation, alert stack, bottom shell, and safe areas.
2. Open City Overview, select Market, select Grain, and inspect stock/reserve/demand/price/status plus causal detail.
3. Open an alert in Inspector and verify cause, remedy, actions, and close restoration.
4. Open Trade Map and traverse the Simple route editor without drag-only input.
5. Open Research from the production HUD, traverse Close and technology nodes, and return focus to the HUD opener.
6. Open Scenario and Save/Load, verify their actionable and unavailable states, and close with Back.
7. Repeat each screen at native 1280x720 and 1920x1080 without post-capture resizing.

## Accessibility and input validation

- Mouse actions continue to use the same presentation intents as semantic/controller activation.
- Keyboard/controller parity is covered by `Hansa.UI.Navigation.KeyboardControllerParity`: Tab/arrows/d-pad move, Shift+Tab/d-pad reverse, Enter/Space/A activate, and Escape/B back out with wrapping.
- `Hansa.UI.Research.SemanticsAndControllerFocus` proves production-HUD reachability, deterministic initial modal focus, d-pad movement, B close, and opener restoration.
- Focus targets retain the design-system minimum of 48 pixels; alert actions now explicitly use it.
- High-contrast palette pairs and stronger high-contrast focus rings pass `Hansa.UI.Style.ContrastAndSeverity` and `Hansa.UI.Style.TokenContract`.
- Warning and critical states use distinct glyphs/labels in addition to color.
- The 1.5x large-text floor and zero-duration reduced-motion major transition pass the shortage semantic UAT assertions; the native UI uses wrapping, scrolling, and tooltips instead of raster scaling.
- Localization expansion is covered by the existing city long-label and inspector localization tests; new market labels are localized `FText` and preserve full meanings in tooltips and semantics.
