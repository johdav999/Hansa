# TR-10 — City privileges, projects, and exceptional control

Status: authoritative gameplay, authored catalog, persistence, and native review projection implemented; focused automation verified 2026-09-21. Native confirmation-button binding and real viewport UAT remain blocked follow-ups and are not claimed here.

## Delivered contract

`AdditionalCommercialPlot` is a city-issued scoped privilege available from Privileged Presence. It costs 120,000 pfennig and 8,000 plank milli-units from the recipient house and its trade-station inventory. The effect is an additional bounded lease at the authored city coordinates with Storage and Commercial categories. The privilege is reversible only while that lease is empty; revocation deactivates the granted lease. The issuer city, recipient house, reviewed authority revision, grant tick, duration/permanence, and status are authoritative state.

`PublicGranary` is a funded partnership project. It costs 90,000 pfennig and 6,000 plank milli-units, constructs for three authoritative ticks, and then increases the city's `Good.Bread` desired reserve by 20,000 exactly once. Funding, completion, goods, money, project status, tick, command event, save state, and fingerprint are authoritative.

Exceptional governance is not earned from volume. The transition requires all of: Privileged Presence, an authored city policy that permits transfer, the active scenario in that policy's allow-list, the exact charter ID, and the reviewed authority revision. Hamburg permits `Charter.Hamburg.FoundingCouncil` in `Scenario.LubeckGrainShortageV1`; Rostock does not permit governance and rejects the same forged command without mutation.

The transfer contract is deliberately narrow: the presence receives the authored Exceptional Governance stage and capabilities. Existing city assets, plots, buildings, roads, routes, debts, market inventories, policies, and station inventory do not transfer. No goods or money move during the charter transition, and leased construction remains bounded by its ordinary authority.

## UI inventory and states

The existing native trade-map presence panel is the review surface. Its dynamic text is authored in Slate/presentation code, not rasterized. It exposes:

- commercial presence status and autonomy disclaimer;
- privilege cost, issuer/recipient, bounded effect, active state, and empty-plot reversal condition;
- project cost/effect and available, funded/constructing, or completed state;
- governance charter/gate state and the explicit non-transfer ownership contract;
- semantic status nodes `TradeMap.Presence.Commercial`, `.Privilege`, `.Project`, and `.Governance`, preserving non-color accessibility meaning.
