# Brewery oak base color

- Status: production texture source
- Generator mode: built-in ImageGen edit
- Model: not exposed
- Native size: 1536 × 1536
- Physical coverage: approximately 1 × 1 metre
- Intended consumers: individual beams, doors, shutters, barrels and hoist parts
- Revision: v2; removed plank seams from v1

## Final prompt

Make the image one continuous aged oak face suitable for mapping onto individual beams and door boards. Remove every vertical plank seam, board boundary, deep straight split, repeated panel edge and join line; replace them with uninterrupted aligned oak grain. Keep the square format, dark warm brown and restrained silvering, fine vertical grain, uniform diffuse albedo-like lighting and subtle small checks. No cross-grain, cast shadows, highlights, AO, vignette, border or watermark.

## QA

- Inspected at native resolution.
- Continuous aligned grain with no board grid.
- No resampling performed.
