## Final prompt

Create a single isolated portrait-format game UI component reference for Hansa: a Foreign Presence progression inspector, 1024x1536. Use dark navy backing, warm parchment cards, oxidized brass frame and dividers, muted teal positive state, terracotta warning state, ivory high-contrast text, restrained late-medieval Hanseatic material detail, crisp native-widget geometry. The component shows Rostock, current Trade Station stage, active status, the next Merchant Office stage, exact progress rows for lawful volume, deliveries, transaction value, shortage relief, reliable operating ticks, solvent ticks, and qualifying investment. Each row has current/required numbers, a simple icon, shape or label in addition to color, and clear met/unmet state. Below, show concrete unlocks: +50 storage units and +4 standing-order slots, then a review/funding card with 150,000 pfennig, 12 planks, 2 tools, 3 construction ticks, and a strong brass-framed action. Include compact eligible, blocked, unaffordable, suspended and under-construction treatments plus a short chronological history. No map, no other panels, no baked production asset text requirements, no fantasy filigree, no glassmorphism, no gradients, no excessive gold, no tiny unreadable type. Leave generous localization space and visible keyboard/controller focus treatment.
