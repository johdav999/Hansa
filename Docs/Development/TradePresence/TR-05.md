## Settlement contract

Orders use a total lifetime purchase budget, with edits preserving already spent money. Price thresholds are deferred to TR-08. Orders execute sequentially by stable station and order identity at market-update boundaries, before ordinary market price/report recalculation. Existing reservations and protected stock remain unavailable to orders. No public stock or money is reserved between updates. Each update settles physical transfers and money together; later orders see committed earlier results. Ship presence is irrelevant. Route integration remains TR-06.

## Implementation and player behavior

`FHansaManageStationOrderCommand` uses the ordinary authority gateway, with owner, active station, presence capability, city policy, accepted good, report, capacity, and integer-range validation. Order IDs are stable within their station; cancellation retains the ID and history. Editing keeps the original good/direction and lifetime spend. Changing these requires a new order. Closure cancels all orders and a closed station remains saveable after its presence/lease link is released.

The native Slate station inspector exposes good/direction, target or reserve, per-update cap, total purchase budget, selection/new order, save, pause/resume, and cancellation. It shows the available report and its age, executed quantities and money, next eligible update, and causal remedies. Absent reports say unavailable. Invalid selection actions and exhausted adjustment bounds are disabled. Keyboard/controller semantic focus reveals controls in the scrolling inspector and preserves their identities during updates.

Stable priority is ascending station ID, then ascending order ID, with ascending market inventory ID inside settlement. Targets use physical station stock. Sales preserve the maximum reserve across all live sale orders for that good, including paused orders, in addition to existing ledger reservations/protection. Every execution uses a candidate inventory ledger and commits goods and money together. Receipts retain movement-sequence bounds and correlated command/station/order identities. The last 16 evaluations are retained per order; cancelled orders no longer append history. Completed means the target/reserve is currently satisfied; recurring evaluation resumes when stock changes.

Purchase prices apply existing transaction friction to the current market price before the market update: unit price rounds half away from zero; purchase totals round upward, sale proceeds toward zero. The default 500-basis-point friction is reduced by the existing authored research effect. Orders never imply route delivery or future prices. This step does not alter legacy route settlement.
